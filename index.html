Cat Math

The bag says scoop daily, and the box count was the real argument all along.

Boundary: illustrative arithmetic only Cat Math does counting arithmetic with labeled care norms - one box per cat plus one, about 20 pounds of clumping litter per cat per month, a 2 to 3 inch depth. Your cat's habits and your vet win every tie. This page never gives a health verdict.

The box truth

The rule is one litter box per cat, plus one. Two cats ask 3 boxes - and the cat that started going beside the box was doing arithmetic, not acting out.

The litter truth

About 20 pounds of clumping litter per cat per month. Two cats go through 40.0 pounds a month - 1.0 of a 40-pound bag - so the monthly run to the store was never optional.

The depth truth

Keep the litter 2 to 3 inches deep. Shallower than 2 and it cannot bury; deeper than 3 and you are buying litter for the landfill and the hallway.
